To My Love ❤️

♡

Happy 2nd anniversary my love. I honestly can't believe that we have already reached two years together. When I look back at everything we have experienced together I realize just how many memories we have created. Two years may sound like a long time but somehow it feels like it went by so quickly. I still remember the beginning of our relationship and all the moments that slowly brought us closer together. Since then you have become such an important part of my life and I honestly cannot imagine these past two years without you.

I want you to know how thankful I am for you. Thank you for all the time you have given me. Thank you for listening to my stories even when they are random. Thank you for laughing with me even when my jokes aren't funny. Thank you for being there during the good days and even during the days when things weren't so easy. There are so many little things you have done that may seem simple but mean so much to me. Those little moments are some of the things I will always remember.

Our relationship has not always been perfect and I know that we have had our misunderstandings and difficult moments. We have had times when we didn't completely understand each other and moments when emotions got in the way. But even through those moments I am thankful that we continued choosing each other. Those experiences taught me that love isn't only about the happy moments. Love is also about patience. It is about understanding. It is about learning from each other and becoming better together.

When I think about everything we have experienced together I realize how many memories we already have. Every conversation. Every laugh. Every date. Every random picture. Every late night conversation. Every silly moment. Every simple day where we were just happy being together. All of those moments became part of our story. They may not seem like huge events to anyone else but they mean something special to me because I experienced them with you.

There are so many things that I love about you. Some of them are things I tell you all the time while others are things that I probably don't say enough. I love the way you make me smile. I love the way you can make an ordinary day feel special. I love the memories we have created and I love knowing that there is someone in my life who I can share both the exciting moments and the simple moments with.

After two years I still choose you. I still want to learn more about you. I still want to hear your stories. I still want to make you laugh. I still want to create memories with you. I still want to experience new places with you and I still want those ordinary days where we don't have to do anything special as long as we are together. I don't know exactly what the future will look like and I don't know what challenges we will face. But I know that I want you to be part of my future. I want to continue growing with you and I want to continue making memories with you.

Thank you for these two beautiful years. Thank you for your patience. Thank you for your love. Thank you for every memory. Thank you for every laugh. Thank you for every moment that made these two years special. If I could go back to the beginning and experience everything again I would still choose you.

I hope that this is only the beginning of our story. I hope that we get to celebrate many more anniversaries together. I hope that years from now we can look back at these memories and smile at how young we were and how much we loved each other. No matter how much time passes I hope you always remember that you are someone incredibly special to me.

Happy 2nd Anniversary ❤️

Two years down. Hopefully a lifetime to go. I love you more than words can explain.

Forever yours,
Carl ❤️
